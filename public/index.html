    Everything is quantized to the grid and limited to the current scale, so nothing lands off beat or off key. Left alone, the stream changes slowly: about one instrument swap a minute, one small arp variation every 16 bars, and a new room only every few minutes. Anything you tap happens faster, and knobs and faders respond instantly. The Shape knobs also turn slowly on their own; touch one to hold it. Edit a layer in the sequencer and it repeats your version until you leave it alone for 3 minutes. Turn Drift up for more change, or switch on Hold to keep what you hear. Use headphones or decent speakers; the sub bass sits around 50 Hz.
